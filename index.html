About Us

Gujrat Mushtarka Goods Transport Co. is a goods transport and logistics company based in Gujrat, Punjab, Pakistan. We work closely with the electric fan manufacturers and pottery producers of Gujrat's industrial area, and carry commercial cargo to cities nationwide.

Our long-standing relationships with local factories and partner transport companies in destination cities help us keep cargo moving. We aim for competitive rates, careful handling and timely dispatch, and we investigate damage claims fairly in accordance with company policy.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which cities do you serve?

    Cities across Pakistan, including Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar, Islamabad and many more, subject to route availability. See Routes & Cities.

    Do you carry small loads?

    Yes. We handle wholesale and small consignments, and can consolidate them with other shipments.

    Can you arrange loading and unloading labour?

    Yes. Tell us what you need on the quote form. Partner companies in destination cities help with unloading.

    Do you offer full truck load and containers?

    Yes, including 40-foot containers where available. Karachi has regular container service.

    Do you move household goods?

    Yes. We arrange vehicles and labour for household shifting.

    What if my goods are damaged?

    Contact us as soon as possible. We investigate damage claims fairly in accordance with company policy.

    How much will it cost?

    Rates depend on route, cargo and vehicle. Send a quote request and we will reply with a quotation.
