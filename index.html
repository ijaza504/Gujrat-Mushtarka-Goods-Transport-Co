About Us

Gujrat Mushtarka Goods Transport Co. is a goods transport and logistics company based in Gujrat, Punjab, Pakistan. We work closely with the electric fan manufacturers and pottery producers of Gujrat's industrial area, and carry commercial cargo to cities nationwide.

Our long-standing relationships with local factories and partner transport companies in destination cities help us keep cargo moving. We aim for competitive rates, careful handling and timely dispatch.

  1. Pickup. We collect goods from your factory or shop.
  2. Loading. Labour is arranged for loading and unloading.
  3. Warehouse. Goods can be stored and consolidated with other shipments.
  4. Dispatch. Cargo travels through our transport network.
  5. Delivery. Partner companies help with unloading, storage where required and final delivery.
